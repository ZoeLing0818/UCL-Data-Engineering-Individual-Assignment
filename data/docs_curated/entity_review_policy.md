# Entity Review Policy

## Purpose
This document defines the review policy for the prototype risk review support system for operational entity monitoring.

The system is designed to support prioritisation and screening, not to produce definitive legal, regulatory, or criminal determinations. Any entity classified as `High`, `Medium`, or `Low` priority should be interpreted as a review-support output rather than a final judgement.

## Review Scope
The prototype focuses on operational business entities that appear active and suitable for entity-level monitoring. The purpose is to identify entities whose profile, disclosure status, or structural characteristics may justify additional scrutiny.

## Review Principles
The review process follows a risk-based approach. Indicators should be interpreted in context and combined with supporting information where available.

The following principles guide review:

1. **Identity and control matter**
   Review should consider who ultimately owns or controls the entity, including persons with significant control and any individuals able to direct the affairs of the entity. Complex company structures may require a structure chart and supporting evidence. Government KYC guidance also highlights the need to identify persons who ultimately own or control the customer’s business and to obtain additional information for complex or offshore structures. 

2. **Recently incorporated entities may require additional scrutiny**
   Recently incorporated UK companies may require additional documentary support, such as a certified extract of the shareholder register. 

3. **Disclosure and filing visibility matter**
   Companies that have been trading for more than 18 months are expected to provide their latest report and accounts where applicable. Missing or unavailable accounts can therefore be relevant to review prioritisation. 

4. **Register reliability and transparency matter**
   The Companies House reform agenda places strong emphasis on identity verification and on improving the reliability and transparency of the register. This supports the use of entity-level completeness and transparency signals in the review process. 5. **Ownership and control can create indirect sanctions exposure**
   An entity may be subject to sanctions restrictions even if it is not itself listed, where it is owned or controlled directly or indirectly by a designated person. Review should therefore remain alert to ownership and control issues. 

## Priority Bands
- **High**: The entity exhibits multiple review indicators and should be prioritised for focused review.
- **Medium**: The entity exhibits some review indicators and should remain in the broader review pool.
- **Low**: The entity currently exhibits fewer review indicators and can remain in the background monitoring population.

## Prototype Limitation
This policy is designed for a prototype review-support workflow. Outputs should be supplemented with additional document checks, ownership/control review, sanctions screening, and external verification where required.
