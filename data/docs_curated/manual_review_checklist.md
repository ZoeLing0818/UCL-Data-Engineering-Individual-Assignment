# Manual Review Checklist

## Purpose
This checklist supports manual review of entities surfaced by the prototype risk review support system.

It is intended for analysts performing focused review of `High`- or `Medium`-priority entities.

---

## Step 1: Confirm Basic Entity Identity
- Confirm entity name, entity ID, legal form, and company status.
- Confirm incorporation date and whether the entity is recently incorporated.
- Check whether the registry profile appears internally consistent.

## Step 2: Check Location Completeness
- Check whether post town and postcode are present.
- If location data is incomplete, note the weakness in profile transparency.
- Where relevant, compare address-related information with any supporting documents or public information.

KYC guidance emphasises proof of address and warns against certain weak or non-residential forms of address evidence. 

## Step 3: Review Filing and Accounts Visibility
- Check whether the entity has filed accounts.
- If `NO ACCOUNTS FILED`, determine whether this is consistent with incorporation timing.
- If the entity appears older but still lacks accounts visibility, escalate for additional review.

## Step 4: Review Charge / Mortgage Structure
- Check whether the entity has outstanding registered charges.
- Check whether the entity also has satisfied or part-satisfied charge history.
- If both are present, treat this as a more complex charge profile.

## Step 5: Review Ownership and Control Risk
- Identify whether there are known or plausible ownership/control concerns.
- Consider whether the entity could be owned or controlled directly or indirectly by another person.
- Where sanctions context is relevant, note that owned-or-controlled entities may be affected even if they are not listed in their own name. 

## Step 6: Review Need for Enhanced Due Diligence
Enhanced review should be considered when:
- the entity is recently incorporated
- accounts visibility is limited
- company structure appears complex
- the entity has weak transparency or incomplete profile information
- there are ownership/control concerns
- there are sanctions-related concerns

KYC guidance highlights the importance of identifying beneficial owners, authorised signatories, complex structures, and source of wealth/source of funds on a risk-based basis. 

## Step 7: Record Review Outcome
Classify the review outcome as one of:
- No further action
- Monitor
- Additional document review required
- Ownership/control review required
- Sanctions review required
- Escalate for enhanced review
